01 · Problem

Pehle kya masla tha?

Before

Har center ki attendance, fees aur homework marking paper aur alag-alag sheets par hoti thi. Head office ko overall report nahi milti thi.

Challenge

Tajweed/Nazra ki marking word-by-word aur rule-by-rule hoti hai, aur students ki audio check karna manual aur time-consuming tha.

Tip: 2-3 lines hi rakhen. Agar purani paper sheet ki photo ho to "Before" ke sath dikha den, comparison bohat effective lagta hai.
